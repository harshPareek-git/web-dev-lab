# Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of the Web Dev Lab project, we are committed to fostering an open and welcoming community. We expect everyone participating in our project to abide by this Code of Conduct. This includes contributors, maintainers, and anyone interacting with the project in any form.

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we pledge to:

- Be respectful and inclusive of diverse backgrounds and perspectives.
- Be considerate and empathetic towards others.
- Listen actively and communicate constructively.
- Be open to feedback and willing to learn from others.
- Show kindness and understanding in all interactions.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards others.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation in any form.
- Offensive, derogatory, or discriminatory comments or jokes.
- Personal attacks or insults, whether directed at individuals or groups.
- Trolling, spamming, or otherwise disrupting discussions.
- Any other conduct that violates the principles of respect and inclusivity.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at [contact@webdevlab.com](mailto:contact@webdevlab.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for other behaviors that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
